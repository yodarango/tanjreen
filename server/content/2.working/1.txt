wanted to eliminate Egypt’s support for the Assyrian Empire and Assyria
control over the area of Palestine and Syria, the spoils of war allowed Baby-
lon to take booty and people from the more minor powers in the region:
such as Syrians, Phoenicians, Egyptians, and Judeans.!
